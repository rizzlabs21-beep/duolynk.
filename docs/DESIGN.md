## Vibe
- Warm editorial romance: soft cream paper, coral ink, lavender cloud shapes and rounded friendly type — greeting-card warmth applied to a product UI.

- Primary: #FF6B6B
- On Primary: #FFFFFF
- Accent: #B8A9FF
- On Accent: #2D2A5E
- Background: #FFF4E8
- Foreground: #2D2A5E
- Muted: #FFFCF7
- Border: #F0DFCE
- Secondary: #FFC857

## Typography
- Heading: Baloo 2 (family: 'Baloo 2', weight: 700, url: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Baloo+2:wght@500;600;700&display=swap)
- Body: Nunito Sans (family: 'Nunito Sans', weight: 400, url: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Nunito+Sans:wght@400;600;700&display=swap)

## Visual Language
- Core visual signature: soft lavender cloud blobs behind hero artwork, a thin coral ribbon curve sweeping beneath the illustration, and small heart and sparkle glyphs scattered at low opacity.
- Material & depth: cream page with paper-white cards; shadows are wide, warm and low-opacity with a coral tint rather than gray; cards lift slightly on hover.
- Containers & buttons: large-radius cards with a warm hairline border; primary buttons are coral pills with white text and a soft coral glow; secondary buttons are paper-white pills with an indigo hairline.
- Layout rhythm: generous vertical breathing room between sections; coral reserved for calls to action and one highlighted word per heading; lavender and gold appear only as small accents, chips and icon tiles.

## Animation
- Entrance: hero copy and artwork fade and rise over 500ms, staggered by 80ms.
- Interaction: cards lift and deepen shadow over 200ms; buttons compress slightly on press.
- Scroll / transition: section headings fade in on intersect over 400ms.

## Forbidden
- No invented statistics, ratings, testimonials or user counts anywhere.
- No blue-to-purple gradients or generic SaaS gradient text.
- No emoji used as UI icons; use lucide icons.

## Additional Notes
- All user-visible copy is English.
- Auth pages use a split screen: cream form panel beside a lavender artwork panel.
- Logo artwork is used exactly as supplied, never recolored or redrawn.
